Import Daily Stock Report

Upload the "Inventory Products Stock Level Report" export (.xlsx/.csv). Matches by SKU and updates stock/RSP for existing products; new SKUs are added.

Import Allowance Tracker (Balances)

Upload the Staff Allowance Tracker .xlsx (its "Summary" sheet). Shows exactly who would change before touching anything — nothing is saved until you click Apply.

Edit Product Pricing

For items on sale on the website but not in the ERP price list — search a product and update its price here.

Import Cin7 RSP Pricing

Upload Cin7's "Products Price List" export (.xlsx). Matches by SKU and updates RSP only, from the real ERP price — not the website's, which can be a markdown or simply unpublished. Stock stays exclusively sourced from the Cin7 stock report; genuinely new SKUs found here are still added (stock starts at 0 until the next stock import picks them up). Promo/bundle/shipping/gift-card rows in this export are automatically skipped.

Import Product Photos

Pulls real photos straight from freedomofmovement.co.za's public product feed and matches them to your catalog by SKU — no file to export or upload. Only products currently live on the website will have a photo available; anything else keeps its category icon on the order screen.

Photo Sync Status

The daily automatic photo sync (pg_cron, runs at 05:00 SAST) logs every run here — this is how you check it's actually working without writing SQL.

RunPagesUpdatedStatusDetail
Import Sale Pricing (Live from Website)

Pulls the same live product feed as Import Product Photos and finds every SKU where the site's price undercuts its "compare at" price — i.e. genuinely on sale right now — and matches it to your catalog by SKU. Overwrites RSP directly, same as the Cin7 pricing import — meaning a later Cin7 import will silently reset it back to full price once the sale ends there too, which is the expected order of operations.

Import Sale Pricing (Markdown, manual paste)

Fallback for a sale that isn't reflected on the website yet, or a one-off list. Paste rows as Style [tab] Discount% [tab] MD RSP, one per line. Matches by exact product name, which is less reliable than the live import above — use that instead when the sale is already live on the site.

Change History

Every manual allowance/balance edit, bulk import, or period reset — who changed it, when, and what it was before. Order-driven balance changes aren't logged here since they already show up in Order History.
