Import Daily Stock Report

Upload the "Inventory Products Stock Level Report" export (.xlsx/.csv). Matches by SKU and updates stock/RSP for existing products; new SKUs are added.

Import Allowance Tracker (Balances)

Upload the Staff Allowance Tracker .xlsx (its "Summary" sheet). Shows exactly who would change before touching anything — nothing is saved until you click Apply.

Edit Product Pricing

For items on sale on the website but not in the ERP price list — search a product and update its price here.

Import Shopify Pricing

Upload a Matrixify products export (.xlsx). Matches by SKU and updates RSP only — Shopify's "Variant Inventory Qty" turned out not to be reliably HQ-specific (it reads as store-wide total, not per-location), so stock stays exclusively sourced from the Cin7 daily report to avoid corrupting availability. Genuinely new Active SKUs are still added (stock starts at 0 until the next Cin7 import picks them up).

Import Sale Pricing (Markdown, manual paste)

Fallback for one-off lists with no Matrixify export handy. Paste rows as Style [tab] Discount% [tab] MD RSP, one per line. Matches by exact product name, which is less reliable than the Shopify import above — use that instead when you can.
